# Code of Conduct

AgentForge follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository security policy. Reports are reviewed confidentially and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For the complete text of the covenant, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
